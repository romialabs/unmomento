# Every play. None of the waiting.

Un Momento turns a full game video into clips of every live play. Timeouts, stoppages and dead time are cut out automatically, and the clips land in your Photos.

1. Pick a game video from your library.
2. Un Momento finds every play.
3. Save the clips to Photos.

## Made for parents on the sideline

Basketball and volleyball first. No editing skills needed, and no uploads: your videos are processed on your iPhone and never leave it.
