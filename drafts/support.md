# Support

Need help? Email [{{ site.data.settings.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.settings.email }}) and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

## Common questions

**Which sports work?**
Basketball and volleyball work best today. Other sports use a general setting and may be less precise.

**My video won't load.**
If the video is stored in iCloud, open it once in Photos so it downloads to your iPhone, then try again.

**Where are my clips?**
Saved clips go to your Photos library. Check Recents.

**How long does it take?**
Usually a few minutes for a full game. Keep the app open while it works.

**Are my videos uploaded?**
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
